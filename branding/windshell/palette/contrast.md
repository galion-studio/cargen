## Graphics only (3:1 UI/graph minimum)

- Signal Orange on Shell Graphite: 4.30:1, fine for marks, lines and large text, not for small text
- Signal Orange on White: 3.05:1, fine for marks, lines and large text, not for small text
- Orange Ink on Graphite: 3.04:1, fine for marks, lines and large text, not for small text

## Never
- Signal Orange for small text or thin lines on Paper/white (2.7:1). Use Orange Ink.
- Sky or Mist as text on Paper/white. Wind Blue or Ink Grey as text on Graphite.
- Sand as text anywhere; it is a surface colour.

## Colour carries meaning on graphs
Signal Orange / Orange Ink = cargen simulation or model; Wind Blue / Sky (and Graphite) = physical test data; Ink Grey / Mist dashed = unvalidated estimate. Never draw an unvalidated number in orange.
